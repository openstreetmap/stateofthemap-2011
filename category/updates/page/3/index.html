One month left: Call for Papers

Time is closing in on this years State of the Map event! In about 4 months we hope to see all of you in Denver. And there is only 30 days left to submit your own maptastick part of SOTM11:

  • Talk – present your paper in a 20 minute slot with time for questions afterwards
  • Posters – display your paper during the conference.
  • Panel Discussion – discuss your paper with a panel of people and the audience.
  • Workshop – up to 2 hour sessions that give OSMers the chance to take part in an in depth tutorial
  • Lightning Talk – 5 minutes to say your piece. More info and sign up later.

Be sure to get your proposal in before it’s too late!

Hello everyone,

The organizing committee for State of the Map 2011  has arranged for airfare discounts with United Airlines and their partners. The discount is valid through nearly the event date, but we suggest you act soon. Fares are going up, and reserving early ensures that you will get the best prices.

Call for Papers: deadline extension

The call for papers deadline has been extended through June 15, 2011, giving more time for great papers to be presented to the selection committee. There has been a great response for topics from several countries and we want to make sure the opportunity to be apart of this years conference is open to those who are still formulating talks.

The OpenStreetMap Foundation invite contributions from mappers, academics, geo-hackers and open geodata supporters around the world. If you are involved in OpenStreetMap mapping, coding or community organisation – or if you want the chance to present your ideas or opinions to the OpenStreetMap community, you should submit a paper to the State of the Map 2011!
